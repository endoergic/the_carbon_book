### Current data anchors (as-of this release)
- Atmospheric CO₂: 425.7 ppm (2025 projected annual mean, GCB 2025)
- Annual fossil CO₂ emissions: 38.1 GtCO₂ (2025 projection, GCB 2025)
- Annual total CO₂ emissions: ~42.2 GtCO₂ (2025 projection, GCB 2025)
- Remaining 1.5°C carbon budget: ~170 GtCO₂ (50% probability, GCB 2025)
- Remaining 2°C carbon budget: ~1,055 GtCO₂ (50% probability, GCB 2025)
- Climate sensitivity (ECS): 2.5–4.0°C "likely" range (IPCC AR6, 2021)

These figures will need updating as new GCB releases (typically November/COP each year) and IPCC assessments are published.
