# Carbon domain reference (conventions, not current facts)

This skill holds the **durable distinctions the book commits to**. It deliberately contains *no* prices, dates, or regulatory states — those rot; verify them with `fact-currency-check`.

## The master frame
- **Two carbons.** Carbon-the-*material* (graphite, coke, the CO₂ molecule, the fossil stock — Track A) vs. carbon-the-*liability* (emissions, accounting, markets, policy — Tracks B/C). Almost all confusion in the field comes from conflating them.
- Apply to every claim: **which carbon, measured how, against what baseline, by whom, how durable?**

## Units (state them, always)
- **tC vs tCO₂** differ by 44/12 ≈ 3.67. "Tons of carbon" is ambiguous — disambiguate.
- **CO₂e** depends on the **GWP horizon** (GWP-100 vs GWP-20); state it. Methane ≈ 28–30 (GWP-100) vs ≈ 80+ (GWP-20).
- **$/tCO₂ vs $/tC**; **gCO₂e/MJ** (carbon intensity); fuel credits are **per-gallon** (RIN, 45Z) vs **per-tonne** (LCFS, VCM) — convert to one basis before comparing/stacking.

## Accounting distinctions
- **Avoided vs reduced vs removed** emissions — not interchangeable; only *removals* physically take carbon back out.
- **Scope 1/2/3**; Scope 3 is usually the majority and the least controllable.
- **Inventory vs target vs claim.**

## Market & integrity concepts
- **Compliance vs voluntary** markets operate under different logic; don't blur them.
- **The four integrity tests:** additionality, baseline, permanence, leakage.
- **Additionality inversion:** the richer the compliance revenue stack, the *weaker* the case for a voluntary credit on top (max commercial viability ⇒ min additionality).
- **Attribute claiming & double counting:** double *issuance* (two credits, one tonne), double *claiming* (one reduction, two goals), double *monetization* (paid twice).
- **Step-function vs continuous-function** fuel standards (RFS/ReFuelEU reward qualifying; LCFS rewards every CI increment).

## Net-zero / corporate
- **Carbon neutral ≠ net zero ≠ science-based ≠ Paris-aligned.** Net zero = deep absolute cuts then *removals* for the residual; the **mitigation hierarchy** is avoid → reduce → neutralize.

## Forward-view concepts
- **CCS ≠ CDR** (point-source capture vs net removal from the atmosphere).
- **Utilization ≠ removal** unless the product is durable (mineralized building materials) or it's clean-energy e-fuel displacing fossil carbon; short-lived products re-release the carbon (the **thermodynamic tax**).
- **Overshoot** and **net-negative dependence**; **permanence tiers** (permanence-by-chemistry > permanence-by-management).
- **Intensity-based vs absolute** caps behave differently.

## Recurring pitfalls to catch
- Treating utilization or avoided-emissions as if it were durable removal.
- Quoting a SAF book-and-claim certificate price as if it were a carbon-abatement price.
- Conflating CCS and CDR, or program *eligibility* with credit *quality*.
- Hardcoding a current price/date instead of sourcing it.

## Honesty norms
Balance critique with the strongest defense; flag genuine uncertainty; prefer "what works / what doesn't / what we don't know" over advocacy.
