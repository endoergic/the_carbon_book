# The Carbon Book

> An open educational resource on the historical, current, and future value chain of carbon — covering both the physical/industrial carbon economy (materials, CO₂ molecule infrastructure, fossil fuels) and the climate-policy/market apparatus built around carbon as a pollutant (emissions accounting, compliance markets, voluntary markets, CDR, corporate disclosure, geopolitics).

## How this book is built

This is a **professional-level introduction** to carbon, written for readers with a working background in an adjacent field — climate, energy, finance, policy, technology — who want comprehensive depth without an undergraduate-textbook ramp.

The structure:

- **Track A — Foundations & Physical Carbon.** The element, the materials, the CO₂ molecule's industrial life, fossil fuels.
- **Track B — Carbon as Climate Liability.** Accounting, policy history, compliance markets, voluntary markets, pricing theory, disclosure.
- **Track C — The Forward View.** CCUS, CDR, hard-to-abate sectors, geopolitics, the speculative frontier.

Chapters are written to be modular. Each declares its prerequisites. The dependency graph allows readers to jump in at the topic they care about and pick up foundations as needed.

## Reading paths

**If you want the full picture in order:** Chapters 1 → 15.

**If you came for the markets:** Chapter 1 (foundation) → Chapter 5 (emissions accounting) → Chapter 7 (compliance markets) → Chapter 8 (voluntary markets) → Chapter 9 (pricing).

**If you came for the physical/industrial side:** Chapter 1 → Chapter 2 (solid carbon commodities) → Chapter 3 (CO₂ molecule value chain) → Chapter 4 (fossil fuels) → Chapter 10 (CCUS).

**If you came for net zero / corporate strategy:** Chapter 1 → Chapter 5 (accounting) → Chapter 8 (voluntary markets) → Chapter 11 (CDR) → Chapter 13 (corporate net zero & disclosure).

**If you came for geopolitics:** Chapter 1 → Chapter 6 (policy history) → Chapter 7 (compliance markets) → Chapter 14 (geopolitics).

## A note on currency

The book references many numbers — atmospheric CO₂ concentration, current carbon prices, annual emissions, remaining carbon budgets, regulatory states — that change. Where a number is meant to be current as of writing, the chapter footer notes the source and as-of date. The `CHANGELOG.md` tracks substantive updates.

Pull requests with corrections, updated figures, or new primary sources are welcome. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
